HORNET HACKS — ONE-PAGE PROJECT CANVAS
======================================

1. THE PROBLEM
What problem are we trying to solve?


2. THE PERSON
Who experiences this problem? Be specific.


3. THE DEMO PROMISE
Finish this sentence:
"Our demo will show that a user can..."


4. THE ONE MUST-HAVE FEATURE
If we only finish one thing, what should work?


5. SAVE FOR LATER
What ideas are useful, but not required for the first demo?


6. TEAM ROLES
Who is leading each area? Roles can overlap and change.
- Build:
- Design:
- Research/content:
- Testing:
- Demo/story:


7. TOOLS
What languages, frameworks, design tools, data, hardware, or APIs will we use?


8. RISKS
What could block us? What is the smallest backup plan?


9. CREDIT CHECK
What outside code, data, media, assets, tutorials, or AI tools must we disclose?


10. DEMO OWNER
Who will run the demo? Who will speak if the demo has a technical issue?


FAST TEST
Can someone new understand the problem, use the key feature, and see why it matters?
