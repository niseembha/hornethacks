About

Build, learn, and launch in one day

HornetHacks is a free, 12-hour hackathon where high school students turn ideas into working projects with support from mentors and peers.

On Saturday, October 10, 2026, students will gather at the Valdes STEM + Innovation Center at Greenhill School to design and build an app, game, website, hardware project, or another original idea. Every team will have the opportunity to present its work at the end of the day.

Participation is free, and no prior experience is required. Beginner workshops and experienced mentors will help students move from idea to demo.

What to expect

Workshops

Practical sessions on web development, APIs, and AI, designed to help beginners make immediate progress.

Mentors

Experienced students and industry volunteers provide technical guidance, design feedback, and presentation support.

Meals and snacks

Breakfast, lunch, and snacks are provided so participants can stay focused throughout the event.

Project showcase

Teams present their work to judges and fellow participants, with awards across multiple project categories.

Who can come

HornetHacks is open to high school students in grades 9–12 from public, private, and home schools across Dallas–Fort Worth and beyond. Teams consist of 2–4 students. Participants may register with a team or meet teammates at the event.

The venue

The event will take place in the Valdes STEM + Innovation Center at Greenhill School, 4141 Spring Valley Rd, Addison, TX 75001. The facility offers collaborative workspaces, maker resources, and room for team presentations. Get directions.
