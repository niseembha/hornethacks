FAQ

Good questions

Everything parents, teachers, and hackers usually ask. Something missing? Ask us directly.

Who can participate?

Any high school student in grades 9–12, from any school — public, private, or homeschool. You don't need to attend Greenhill to hack at HornetHacks.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. HornetHacks is completely free thanks to our sponsors — that includes meals, snacks, swag, and prizes.

Do I need coding experience?

Nope. A big part of HornetHacks is built for first-timers: beginner workshops, roaming mentors, and prize categories just for first hacks. Curiosity is the only prerequisite.

How do teams work?

Teams are 1–4 students. Bring a ready-made team, or join the team-formation mixer right after opening ceremony — plenty of great teams meet that morning.

What should I bring?

A laptop and charger are the essentials. Headphones, a water bottle, and a student ID are smart additions. Building hardware? Bring your kit — we'll have some components and tools on hand too.

Will there be food?

Yes — breakfast, lunch, and all-day snacks are provided. You'll be able to note dietary restrictions when you register.

Is it an overnight event?

No. HornetHacks is a one-day, 12-hour event: doors open at 8:00 AM and awards wrap by 8:00 PM the same Saturday. No sleeping bags required.

What can I build?

Anything you can make in a day: websites, mobile apps, games, hardware hacks, data projects, AI experiments. Projects must be started (from scratch) at the event.

Do parents or guardians need to sign anything?

Yes — a permission and photo-release form comes with registration. Parents and guardians are also welcome at the evening project expo and awards.

How do I sign up?

Registration opens soon. For now, join the interest list and we'll email you the moment it's live.

Still curious?

We answer fast. Reach out, or just join the interest list and follow along.
