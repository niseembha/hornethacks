FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Essential information for students, families, and educators. Need something else? Contact the team.

Who can participate?

Any high school student in grades 9–12, from any school — public, private, or homeschool. You don't need to attend Greenhill to hack at HornetHacks.

How much does it cost?

HornetHacks is free for all participants. Sponsorship covers meals, snacks, event materials, and prizes.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Beginner workshops, mentors, and entry-level award categories are designed to support first-time participants.

How do teams work?

Teams consist of 2–4 students. You may register with a team or meet teammates during team formation at the event.

What should I bring?

Bring a laptop, charger, student ID, headphones, and a reusable water bottle. Participants planning a hardware project should also bring any specialized components they expect to use.

Will there be food?

Yes — breakfast, lunch, and all-day snacks are provided. You'll be able to note dietary restrictions when you register.

Is it an overnight event?

No. HornetHacks is a one-day event running from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on Saturday, October 10, 2026.

What can I build?

You may build a website, mobile app, game, hardware project, data tool, AI application, or another original project. All project work must begin at the event.

What is the Google Gemini Track?

The Google Gemini Track recognizes projects that use Gemini to solve a clear problem or create a useful experience. Resources, judging criteria, and award details will be announced before registration opens.

Do parents or guardians need to sign anything?

Yes — a permission and photo-release form comes with registration. Parents and guardians are also welcome at the evening project expo and awards.

How do I sign up?

Registration opens soon. For now, join the interest list and we'll email you the moment it's live.

Need more information?

Contact the organizing team or join the interest list for event announcements.
