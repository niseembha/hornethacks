Sponsors

Power the hive

HornetHacks runs entirely on the generosity of sponsors. Your support puts laptops-open, hands-on STEM in front of hundreds of high school students — and your brand at the center of it.

Why sponsor

Meet tomorrow's builders

Connect with motivated, technically curious students years before recruiters do — and their families and teachers, too.

Real, visible impact

Sponsorship pays for meals, prizes, workshop materials, and swag. Every dollar shows up in a student's day.

Be part of the story

Logo placement, an opening-ceremony shoutout, the option to send mentors or judges, and a demo-day front seat.

How it works

We offer sponsorship tiers for every budget — from covering a snack table to presenting the whole event — plus in-kind options like prizes, credits, and swag. Tell us a bit about your organization and we'll send over the full sponsorship packet.

Sponsors are welcome (and encouraged!) to send mentors, judges, and workshop hosts on event day. There's no better way to meet the hive.

Become a sponsor

Two minutes of your time, twelve hours of student building. Tell us you're interested and we'll take it from there.
